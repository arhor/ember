# Research

Research syntheses preserve conclusions, judgments, hypotheses, evidence limits and
open questions. [Accepted ADRs](../architecture/decisions/README.md) own adopted choices; a recommendation,
benchmark or prototype alone does not accept an architecture or production dependency.
Evidence maps connect conclusions to durable references; dated reports remain observations
of the recorded versions/environments, not current operating instructions.

Use [guides](../guides/README.md) to reproduce evaluations and
[the archive](archive/README.md) for original reports and superseded investigations.
Do not upgrade hypotheses or correlated sources into accepted facts or independent evidence.
